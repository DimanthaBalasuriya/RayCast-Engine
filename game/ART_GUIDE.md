# Weapon art workflow (Phase 3 M0a)

The game currently uses rear-perspective PX4 pistol art and the A1 assault rifle design in the shotgun gameplay slot. Fire, reload, switch and pump use distinct frames that can be refined with hand-drawn in-between poses. The original placeholder weapon images were removed. No separate prop or item placeholder images were present in `assets/`.

1. Open `art_templates/weapons_template.png` for labels and guides. Paint on top of `art_templates/weapons_blank.png` in a layer-capable editor. The half-transparent `art_templates/weapons_reference_overlay.png` shows the current concept-derived art; keep it on a separate tracing layer and hide it before export.
2. Keep every frame in its labeled **96×96 px** cell. Draw the held gun from behind, with its barrel pointing into the screen toward the crosshair; use side profiles only for HUD icons. The bottom-center cross is the sheet guide. Keep art within the inset safe border where possible. Draw neutral lighting from upper left; leave muzzle flash and smoke out of gun frames. Extra cells contain each 32×16 HUD icon, two 32×32 flash sprites, and a 96×96 aim-down-sights (`ads`) view per weapon. The rifle scope needs a transparent opening.
3. Export an **RGBA** sheet at **768×480 px** as `assets/weapons.png`. Copy `art_templates/weapons_template.json` to `assets/weapons.json`. Keep its rectangles intact unless the sheet layout changes. `offset` is a per-frame pixel nudge. `hip_sight` marks the front sight in a frame and `hip_sight_drop` places it below the crosshair. `ads_sight` marks the aimed sight center; `ads_scale` enlarges its sprite. `muzzle_offset` places the hip-fire flash, and `frame_time` is seconds per frame. `pickup_sprite` is optional.
4. Run `python tools/validate_weapons.py`. Fix every error. Warnings indicate art touching a cell edge or a possible alignment jump. Preview with `python tools/preview_weapons.py --viewer`. Use Left/Right for weapons, Up/Down for animations, `,`/`.` to step, Space to pause, A for anchor, B for three background brightness levels, and `+`/`-` to zoom.
5. Run `python main.py`. Check firing, reload, pump, switch, HUD icon and flash in bright and dark areas. Missing or empty exported art logs one warning per item and falls back to `assets/weapons_concept.png`.

`python tools/weapon_template.py` only generates a new template if files do not exist. `--force` backs up existing templates first. `python tools/generate_weapons.py` is a legacy generator; it refuses to overwrite art unless `--force` and backs up files before overwriting. Do not run it to make final art. Concept sheets live in `art_concepts/weapon_pack_v1/`.
